ABOUT V CHALLENGE

Nurturing Academic Excellence in Dharwad

Located at Girinagar, near Aryabhata PU College on Kalaghatagi Road, Dharwad, V Challenge Academy is built on a foundation of discipline, quality, and comprehensive student preparation.

ಧಾರವಾಡದ ಕಾಲಘಟಗಿ ರಸ್ತೆಯ ಆರ್ಯಭಟ ಪಿಯು ಕಾಲೇಜು ಎದುರಿನ ಗಿರಿ ನಗರದಲ್ಲಿರುವ ನಮ್ಮ ಸಂಸ್ಥೆಯು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳ ಉಜ್ವಲ ಭವಿಷ್ಯಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಗುಣಮಟ್ಟದ ಶಿಕ್ಷಣ ನೀಡುತ್ತಿದೆ.

  • Experienced Faculty: Highly skilled trainers focused on individual mental agility and problem-solving skills.
  • Dedicated Batches: Separate focused groups for boys and girls with individualized attention.
  • Boarding & Food: Clean, home-like hygienic food and purified drinking water facility on campus.
  • Bilingual Coaching: Seamless instruction in both Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) and English mediums.

Why Choose V Challenge?

We combine rigorous mock-test discipline with personal care, ensuring every child achieves their full potential for competitive entrance exams.
